About me

Hi, I'm Johnny. I'm a senior at CU Boulder's Leeds School of Business studying Finance and Computer Science Integration, and I graduate this December. I grew up in Austin, Texas, and came to Colorado to run Division I track and field, which I competed in before shifting my focus to school and work.

Right now I'm a supply chain intern at AMP Robotics, a company that builds AI-driven systems for sorting recyclable waste. A lot of my work there involves modeling equipment costs and building reporting pipelines, which is where my Alteryx and spreadsheet background gets used hardest. I'm taking this class because I've built plenty of things that work and very few that are genuinely accessible, and I'd like to close that gap before I graduate. Sko Buffs!
